# Business Analyst Portfolio

Four case studies from a simulated embedded BA engagement at **Brightpath Distribution**, a mid-size regional distributor. Where the [data analytics portfolio](../README.md) answers "what does the data say," this one answers "what should we do about it, and how do we actually get it done" — the deliverables here are the artifacts a business analyst actually produces: process maps, a BRD, a stakeholder analysis, a business case. Full narrative, tools, and figures for each case in its own README.

**Website:** [dukeolu.github.io](https://dukeolu.github.io/) — Business Analyst track.

## Case studies

| # | Case | Type | Deliverables |
|---|------|------|--------------|
| 01 | [Cutting AP invoice approval time without cutting corners](case-01-process-improvement/) | Process improvement / workflow redesign | Process improvement charter, current vs. future-state process map, cycle-time workbook |
| 02 | [Choosing a CRM the sales team will actually use](case-02-crm-selection/) | Requirements gathering & systems selection | Business requirements document + traceability matrix, weighted vendor evaluation |
| 03 | [Integrating two warehouse teams without it reading as a takeover](case-03-change-management/) | Change management / stakeholder alignment | Stakeholder power/interest analysis, RACI matrix, change management plan |
| 04 | [Automate or keep hiring? A business case for order entry](case-04-business-case/) | Business case & cost-benefit | Business case with risk register, 3-year NPV/payback model |

Each case follows the same spine: business problem → method → key finding → recommendation & business impact, and every numeric claim is backed by a formula-driven Excel workbook (verified with zero formula errors via LibreOffice recalculation) rather than a number typed into a document by hand.

## Build note

Brightpath Distribution, its people, and every dataset and dollar figure in this portfolio are simulated — built for this portfolio, not pulled from a real engagement, and disclosed as such in every deliverable. The BA methodology, the workbook formulas, and the documents themselves are real and reproducible: each case's scripts folder regenerates its artifacts from scratch. See each case's README for its specific limitations.
