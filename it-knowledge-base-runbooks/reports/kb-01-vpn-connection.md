# Connecting to the Office VPN (Windows &amp; Mac)

**Applies to:** All remote and hybrid employees
**Estimated time to fix:** 2–5 minutes
**If this doesn't work:** Skip to "Still stuck?" at the bottom — don't spend more than 10 minutes on this yourself.

---

## Before you start

Check these two things first — they cause more VPN problems than anything else:

1. **Is your regular internet working?** Try loading a website. If the internet itself is down, the VPN isn't the problem.
2. **Is your VPN client up to date?** If you haven't restarted your laptop in more than a week, do that first — a pending update is the single most common cause of "VPN won't connect" tickets.

## Windows

1. Click the network icon in the bottom-right system tray.
2. Select **Meridian VPN** from the list of connections.
3. Click **Connect**, then enter your regular network username and password (the same one you use to log into your laptop).
4. Wait up to 30 seconds — the first connection after a Windows update is often slower than usual.
5. You'll see a lock icon appear next to the network icon once you're connected.

**If you get "Error 809" or "Error 812":** This almost always means your Windows firewall or a hotel/cafe wifi network is blocking the VPN port. Try switching to your phone's hotspot — if that works, the problem is the network you're on, not your laptop or your account.

## Mac

1. Open **System Settings** → **VPN**.
2. Select **Meridian VPN** and click **Connect**.
3. Enter your regular network username and password when prompted.
4. Look for the VPN icon in the menu bar to confirm you're connected.

**If the VPN app won't open at all:** Check **System Settings** → **Privacy & Security** — macOS sometimes silently blocks the VPN app after a system update until you approve it there.

## Still stuck?

If you've tried the steps above and it's still not connecting, **submit a ticket** and include:
- Windows or Mac, and which OS version
- The exact error message or code, if you got one
- Whether you're on home wifi, a hotel/public network, or the office network

This gets your ticket to the right technician faster than "VPN doesn't work" — most VPN tickets that include an error code get resolved in one round of back-and-forth instead of three.
