# Fixing Common Printer Problems Before You Call the Help Desk

**Applies to:** All office printers/MFPs
**Estimated time to fix:** 2–5 minutes
**If this doesn't work:** Skip to "Still stuck?" at the bottom.

---

## "Printer shows offline" or won't print at all

This is the #1 printer complaint, and it's usually one of three things, in order of how often each one is actually the cause:

1. **The printer went to sleep and needs a moment.** Walk over and check if the screen is dark. Tap it — if it wakes up, try printing again after 15 seconds.
2. **You're printing to the wrong printer.** Open your print dialog and double-check the printer name matches the one you're standing next to — it's easy to print to a printer on another floor by accident, and that shows up as "offline" from where you are.
3. **The print queue is jammed from an earlier failed job.** On Windows: Settings → Printers & Scanners → click the printer → Open Queue → cancel anything stuck at the top → try again. On Mac: System Settings → Printers & Scanners → click the printer → clear anything stuck in the queue.

## "Paper jam" that won't clear

1. Open every access panel the printer has (front, back, and top tray) — jams often have a second sheet stuck somewhere you can't see from the first panel you check.
2. Pull jammed paper in the direction it normally feeds, not backward — pulling backward is the most common way a small jam turns into a service call.
3. Once clear, close every panel firmly until you hear it latch — an unlatched panel will make the printer refuse to print even after the jam is gone.

## Print quality problems (streaks, faded print, wrong colors)

This is almost always a toner/ink issue, not a network issue. Check the printer's own display for a low-toner warning first — most of our printers show this before quality actually degrades, so if you're already seeing streaks, the cartridge may need replacing sooner than the display suggests.

## Still stuck?

**Submit a ticket** and include:
- The printer's name/location (e.g., "3rd floor east, near the kitchen")
- What exactly happens (error message, blank pages, wrong output, etc.)
- Whether it just started, or has been intermittent for a while

If it's affecting the whole floor, not just your print job, say so in the ticket — that changes how urgently it gets picked up.
