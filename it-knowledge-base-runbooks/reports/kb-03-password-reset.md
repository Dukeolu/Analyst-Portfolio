# Resetting Your Network Password Yourself

**Applies to:** All employees
**Estimated time to fix:** Under 2 minutes
**If this doesn't work:** Skip to "Still stuck?" at the bottom.

---

## If you know your current password but it's about to expire

You'll get a warning banner starting 10 days before expiration. Click it, or go directly to **passwordreset.meridianmfg.com**, and follow the prompts — you'll need your current password once more to set the new one.

## If you've forgotten your password entirely

1. Go to **passwordreset.meridianmfg.com** from any device (your phone is fine if your laptop is locked).
2. Enter your username (the part of your email before the @).
3. Choose to verify by text message or your personal backup email — whichever you set up when you joined.
4. Set a new password. It must be at least 12 characters and include a number — a short phrase like `CorrectHorseBattery9` is both easier to remember and harder to guess than `P@ssw0rd1`.
5. Your new password takes effect everywhere (laptop, email, VPN, timeclock) within about 2 minutes.

## If you never set up a backup verification method

This is the one case self-service can't handle — Meridian requires a technician to verify your identity in person or over a known phone number before resetting a password with no backup method on file, for security reasons. Submit a ticket and expect this to take longer than a self-service reset.

## Account locked after too many failed attempts?

This is a separate issue from a forgotten password — if you're seeing "account locked," wait 15 minutes and try again with your correct password before assuming you need a reset. Repeated failed attempts lock the account temporarily as a security measure, not because the password itself was invalidated.

## Still stuck?

**Submit a ticket** and include whether you have a backup verification method on file (check the "if you never set up" section above) — that's the single detail that determines whether this can be fixed in under 5 minutes or needs an in-person identity check.
