# Setting Up Email on Your Phone

**Applies to:** iPhone and Android, personal or company-issued
**Estimated time to fix:** 5–10 minutes
**If this doesn't work:** Skip to "Still stuck?" at the bottom.

---

## iPhone

1. Open **Settings** → **Mail** → **Accounts** → **Add Account**.
2. Choose **Microsoft Exchange** (not "Other," even though Meridian email looks like a regular inbox — picking the wrong account type here is the #1 reason this setup fails).
3. Enter your full Meridian email address, then tap **Sign In**.
4. You'll be redirected to a Meridian sign-in page — enter your regular network password there, not on the first screen.
5. If prompted for multi-factor approval, check your Authenticator app and approve the sign-in.
6. Toggle on **Mail**, **Contacts**, and **Calendars**, then tap **Save**.

## Android

1. Open the **Gmail** app (Meridian email works through the standard Gmail app, even though it's not a Gmail account) or your phone's built-in **Email** app.
2. Choose **Add account** → **Exchange and Office 365**.
3. Enter your full Meridian email address and tap **Next**.
4. On the Meridian sign-in page that opens, enter your network password.
5. Approve the multi-factor prompt if asked.
6. Accept the "Remote security administration" permission prompt — this is required for company email on Android and is not a sign anything is wrong; it lets IT remotely wipe just the email data (not your personal apps or photos) if the phone is ever lost.

## Common problem: "Account setup can't be completed"

This almost always means the multi-factor approval expired before you finished — the prompt only stays valid for about 60 seconds. Delete the partially-added account and start over, but have your Authenticator app already open on a second device (or already unlocked) before you begin step 3, so you can approve it the moment it appears.
